The flagship · automation

Every appointment becomes a follow-up — automatically.

The moment a visit is marked complete, AIKABZ picks the right treatment-specific sequence, writes each message in your brand voice, and schedules it for the perfect moment — in the client's timezone.

Example: A Botox client leaves today. AIKABZ doesn't text "thanks for visiting." It waits 14 days — when results are actually visible — then sends a check-in that references the treatment, the provider, and a one-tap rebook link.
  • Treatment-aware timing (Botox → day 14, microneedling → day 3)
  • Quiet hours respected — nothing sends at 9 PM
  • Frequency-capped — never more than 3 messages in 24h
  • You can preview & edit any message before it goes out

Protects your big revenue

Multi-session protocols, tracked to the finish.

Laser hair removal is 6 sessions. Most clients quit after 2 — the single biggest source of revenue leakage in a spa. AIKABZ knows exactly which session each client is on and nudges them to book the next one at the clinically optimal interval.

Example: Dana finished session 3 of 6 today. AIKABZ recalculates her ideal next date from today — not the original plan — and will nudge her 7 days before it's due.
  • Knows total sessions, optimal interval & tolerance per treatment
  • Re-times the whole plan if a client slips
  • Providers see "Session 3 of 6" right on the chart
  • Booked-in-protocol clients are 4× more likely to finish
